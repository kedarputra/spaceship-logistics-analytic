Palette, type and parts

Layout: a dark rail with an accent bar on the active item, white panels on a warm ground, small radii, tabular numbers. The three brand colours come from the brief; chart marks are deeper steps of them, checked for colour-blind separation.

Sage · #AFD0BFTints, question chips, the ground of icon squares, small-sample bars of a green series. Never text.
Grey-green · #808F87Icons and secondary strokes. 3.4:1 on white: enough for icons, not for body text.
Amber · #F4B266The Ask button, the active menu item, the forecast band. Ink text on it reads at 9:1.
